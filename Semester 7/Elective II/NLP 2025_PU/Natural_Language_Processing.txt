Once upon a time, an old mother pig had three little pigs. She didn’t have enough food to take care of them, so when they were old enough, she sent them out into the world to find their own way.

The first little pig was very lazy. He didn’t want to put in any effort, so he built his house from straw.
The second little pig tried a bit harder, but he was still somewhat lazy. He decided to build his house from sticks. After they finished their houses, the two pigs sang, danced, and played for the rest of the day.

The third little pig was hardworking. He spent the whole day building his house out of bricks. His house was strong and solid, complete with a fireplace and chimney. It looked sturdy enough to withstand even the strongest winds.
